# Bally Druid Shift

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

**Shift out and back into your current Druid form with one button.**

When roots or slows get in your way, Bally Druid Shift helps you refresh your current form to remove movement-impairing effects that shapeshifting can break. It follows your learned forms, so you can keep using one action-bar button as you change forms.

### Get started

1. Enable the addon and log in to your Druid.
2. Open `/macro`, find **BLHShift** under character-specific macros, and drag it to your action bar.
3. Press it while in a recognized form to cancel and recast that form. It does nothing in humanoid or unrecognized forms.

The addon updates its macro when your available forms change and preserves same-named custom macros. If the macro name conflicts or your character macro list is full, a chat message will tell you what needs attention.

Shapeshifting still follows game rules: some effects cannot be removed this way, and insufficient mana or a restriction on casting may leave you in humanoid form.

## 简体中文

**一个按键，取消并重新变回当前德鲁伊形态。**

被定身或减速拖住时，Bally Druid Shift 帮你重新变形，解除可通过变形移除的移动限制。插件会跟随已学会的形态调整，无论切换到哪种已识别形态，都可以继续使用同一个技能栏按键。

### 开始使用

1. 启用插件并登录德鲁伊角色。
2. 输入 `/macro`，在**角色专用宏**中找到 **BLHShift**，拖到技能栏。
3. 处于已识别形态时按下宏，即可尝试取消并重新施放当前形态；人形或未识别形态下不会执行操作。

学会或改变可用形态后，插件会更新宏，并保留同名的自定义宏。若宏名称冲突或角色宏已满，聊天框会给出提示。

变形仍遵循游戏规则：部分控制效果无法通过变形解除；法力不足或受到施法限制时，角色可能停留在人形。
